FAQ

Questions, answered

Is PYQ Bundle free?

Yes. With a free account you can practise PYQs, use topic-wise practice, make notes and see your performance analytics. The free plan includes ads and one exam course at a time. Premium removes ads, lets you add more courses and syncs your notes across devices.

Which exams can I practise?

SSC GD Constable, SSC CGL, RRB NTPC, RRB JE, JEE Main, NEET UG, UPSC Civil Services and CUET UG. We add previous papers and topics regularly, so coverage varies by exam. See all exams.

Is there an Android app?

The PYQ Bundle Android app is coming soon to Google Play. Until then, you can practise online, which also works in your phone's browser.

Yes. Download PYQ Bundle on Google Play. It works on Android 7.0 or later, and you can also practise online.

Do I need an account?

Yes. A free account saves your practice history, stats and progress. You can sign up with Google or with your email address.

How do I contact you?

Email pyqbundle@gmail.com or visit our contact page.
