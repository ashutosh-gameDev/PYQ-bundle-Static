Yes. Create a free PYQ Bundle account to practise CUET UG previous year questions online, subject-wise, topic-wise or as full papers, and see your performance analytics. The free plan includes ads and one exam course at a time; Premium removes ads, lets you add more courses and syncs your notes.

What is the CUET UG exam pattern?

Exam pattern (recent): Languages (Examples: English and other languages); Domain subjects (Examples: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology and more); General Test (Examples: Reasoning, numerical ability, general knowledge). Each test has 50 compulsory questions in 60 minutes. +5 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong answer. The number of subjects you can choose and other rules may change each year. Exam patterns can change, so always confirm in the latest official notification.

PYQ Bundle currently has 6 CUET UG subjects with 36 topics: General Aptitude Test, English, Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Biology. Previous papers and topics are added regularly, so coverage varies.

Do I need to download CUET UG PYQ PDFs?

Is PYQ Bundle an official CUET UG website?

No. PYQ Bundle is an independent practice platform and is not affiliated with the National Testing Agency (NTA). For official notifications, dates and answer keys, visit nta.ac.in.
