What is the NEET exam pattern?

Exam pattern (recent): Physics (Questions: 45, Marks: 180); Chemistry (Questions: 45, Marks: 180); Biology (Botany + Zoology) (Questions: 90, Marks: 360). Total: Questions 180, Marks 720. Duration: 3 hours (180 minutes). All questions are compulsory. +4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong answer. Exam patterns can change, so always confirm in the latest official notification.
