About SSC CGL

Selection stages:

  1. Tier 1: computer-based qualifying exam
  2. Tier 2: computer-based exam (Paper I for all posts; Paper II Statistics for JSO and Statistical Investigator posts)
  3. Document verification and post-specific skill tests where applicable

Official information: ssc.gov.in

What is the SSC CGL exam pattern?

Tier 1 (recent pattern): General Intelligence & Reasoning (Questions: 25, Marks: 50); General Awareness (Questions: 25, Marks: 50); Quantitative Aptitude (Questions: 25, Marks: 50); English Comprehension (Questions: 25, Marks: 50). Total: Questions 100, Marks 200. Duration: 60 minutes. 0.50 marks are deducted for each wrong answer. Tier 2 Paper I covers Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge and a Data Entry Speed Test.
