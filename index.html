    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Is PYQ Bundle free?

    Yes. Students will be able to access and practice PYQs for free. An optional Premium plan will provide additional benefits such as an ad-free experience, cloud sync and AI solutions.

    What exams will be available?

    We're starting with selected competitive exams and continuously adding more exams, subjects and previous-year papers.

    What do early testers do?

    Early testers use PYQ Bundle before the public launch, report problems and share feedback that helps us improve the experience.

    How do I get 1 Year Premium free?

    Active early testers who complete the testing requirements and provide useful feedback will receive one year of PYQ Bundle Premium free.

    When is PYQ Bundle launching?

    We're currently preparing for early access. Pre-register and we'll notify you when testing begins.
