JEE Main PYQs: Free Practice Online

JEE Main is the national engineering entrance exam for admission to B.E./B.Tech programmes at NITs, IIITs and other participating institutions. It's also the qualifying exam for JEE Advanced (for IIT admissions). It's held in two sessions each year.

About JEE Main

Selection stages:

  1. Session 1 (January) and Session 2 (April): you can attempt one or both
  2. Your best NTA score is used for ranking

What is the JEE Main exam pattern?

Paper 1 – B.E./B.Tech (recent pattern): Physics (Questions: 25, Marks: 100); Chemistry (Questions: 25, Marks: 100); Mathematics (Questions: 25, Marks: 100). Total: Questions 75, Marks 300. Duration: 3 hours, computer-based. Each subject has 20 multiple-choice and 5 numerical-value questions. +4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong answer. Exam patterns can change, so always confirm in the latest official notification.
