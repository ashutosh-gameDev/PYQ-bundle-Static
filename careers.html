Work with PYQ Bundle

Help students across India practise real exam questions. We're looking for careful, reliable freelancers to add previous year questions to PYQ Bundle.

Open role

Data Entry Freelancer

Apply
  • Freelance
  • Remote · Work from home
  • Flexible hours
  • No manual typing
  • Paid per paper

You'll add previous year question papers to PYQ Bundle using AI tools. There's no manual typing: the AI reads the paper and you check and upload the result.

How the work is done

  1. Copy the promptCopy the ready-made prompt from our CRM.
  2. Ask the AIGive the question paper PDF and the prompt to an AI chat tool such as ChatGPT or Gemini.
  3. Paste the JSONCopy the JSON the AI returns and paste it into the CRM.
  4. Add images if neededIf a question has a diagram or image, upload the PDF in the CRM, select the question, crop the image and upload it.
  5. CheckMake sure the questions, options and answers look right before you submit the paper.

Who we're looking for

  • Comfortable with a computer: copy-paste, uploading files and switching between browser tabs
  • Has used, or can quickly learn, AI chat tools like ChatGPT or Gemini
  • Careful and detail-oriented: one wrong option changes a student's answer
  • A laptop or desktop with a reliable internet connection (a phone isn't enough)
  • Can read English and/or Hindi question papers; familiarity with SSC, Railway, JEE, NEET or UPSC papers is a plus

How hiring works

  1. Apply belowTakes about 2 minutes.
  2. One test paperShortlisted applicants get CRM access and complete one paper to try the process.
  3. Start workingPick up papers and work on your own schedule. You're paid for each completed paper.

Payment is per paper. We'll share the rate per paper with shortlisted applicants. Questions? Email pyqbundle@gmail.com.
