Selection stages:

  1. Preliminary Examination (objective, screening)
  2. Main Examination (9 descriptive papers)

What is the UPSC CSE exam pattern?

Preliminary Examination (recent pattern): General Studies Paper I (Questions: 100, Marks: 200); General Studies Paper II (CSAT) (Questions: 80, Marks: 200). Each paper is 2 hours. CSAT is qualifying (33% needed); merit is based on GS Paper I. One-third of the marks for a question is deducted for each wrong answer. Mains: two qualifying language papers, Essay, General Studies I–IV and two Optional papers, followed by the interview. Exam patterns can change, so always confirm in the latest official notification.
