About RRB NTPC

Selection stages:

  1. CBT 1 (screening, common for all posts)
  2. CBT 2 (level-wise, for the posts you applied for)
  3. Typing Skill Test or Computer Based Aptitude Test, depending on the post
  4. Document verification and medical examination

Official information: rrbapply.gov.in

What is the RRB NTPC exam pattern?

CBT 1 and CBT 2 (recent pattern): General Awareness (CBT 1 questions: 40, CBT 2 questions: 50); Mathematics (CBT 1 questions: 30, CBT 2 questions: 35); General Intelligence & Reasoning (CBT 1 questions: 30, CBT 2 questions: 35). Total: CBT 1 questions 100, CBT 2 questions 120. Duration: 90 minutes for each CBT. One-third of the marks for a question is deducted for each wrong answer. Exam patterns can change, so always confirm in the latest official notification.
