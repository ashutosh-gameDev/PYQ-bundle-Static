What is the RRB JE exam pattern?

CBT 1 (recent pattern): Mathematics (Questions: 30); General Intelligence & Reasoning (Questions: 25); General Awareness (Questions: 15); General Science (Questions: 30). Total: Questions 100. CBT 1 duration: 90 minutes. CBT 2: 150 questions in 120 minutes, with 100 technical questions from your branch plus general awareness, physics & chemistry, computers, and environment & pollution control. One-third of the marks for a question is deducted for each wrong answer. Exam patterns can change, so always confirm in the latest official notification.
