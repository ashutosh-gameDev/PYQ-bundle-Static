SSC GD Constable PYQs: Free Practice Online

SSC GD Constable recruits Constables (General Duty) for the Central Armed Police Forces such as BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP and SSB, Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles, and Sepoy in the NCB. Its computer-based exam tests reasoning, general awareness, elementary maths and English or Hindi at a 10th-pass level.

What is the SSC GD exam pattern?

Computer Based Examination (recent pattern): General Intelligence & Reasoning (Questions: 20, Marks: 40); General Knowledge & General Awareness (Questions: 20, Marks: 40); Elementary Mathematics (Questions: 20, Marks: 40); English / Hindi (Questions: 20, Marks: 40).
